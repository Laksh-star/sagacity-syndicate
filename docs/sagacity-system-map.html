↓ materiality assessment
Non-material

Keep working

“Okay,” status questions, and conversational acknowledgements do not invalidate deliberation.

Ambiguous

Clarify once

Preserve current work and ask a brief question instead of destroying it.

Material

Reflect, do not rerun

Show old and new values when available and wait for explicit confirmation.
